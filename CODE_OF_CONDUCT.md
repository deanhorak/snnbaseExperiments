# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be direct and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Respect differing experience
levels and identities. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
exclude participants whose conduct is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository
owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially
as practical.
